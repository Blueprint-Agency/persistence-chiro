Phase 1 — Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM): test demand through the blog

Next after Month 4

How it works: we publish TCM blog posts on this site and send interested readers to the partner TCM clinic. Nothing changes in the clinic yet. The posts show how much real online demand there is before you decide to open your own TCM service.

  • Every post links to the partner clinic through a tracked link, so each click is counted.
  • The partner logs bookings that came from us (a short referral code or “found you on Persistence” note), so clicks can be matched to real visits.
  • Review after 3 months of the first post going live: search impressions and clicks on the TCM posts, clicks through to the partner, and bookings the partner received. That review is where the decision on your own TCM service gets made.
  • Same rules as the rest of the site: no promises of results, no “treatment” wording, and nothing that repeats the existing Dry Needling vs Acupuncture post.
