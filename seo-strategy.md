  > **⚠️ TITLE AMENDED 2026-08-21.** The prescribed title above is
  > `Chiropractor in Cheras (Maluri), Kuala Lumpur | Persistence Chiropractic` — **72
  > characters**, past the ~60 Google renders, so the brand suffix was truncated on every
  > impression. The build now ships:
  >
  > ```
  > Chiropractor in Cheras, Maluri, KL | Persistence Chiropractic   (61 chars)
  > ```
  >
  > **All three locality terms are kept.** The character budget is paid by abbreviating
  > "Kuala Lumpur" to "KL", not by dropping a locality. Justification: we rank **21.5** for
  > `chiropractor kuala lumpur` *with* the full form in the title, and **17.0** for `chiro kl`
  > *without* "KL" in it — exact-match is not carrying the ranking, and Google resolves the two
  > as one entity.
  >
  > **A rejected intermediate is recorded here as a warning.** An earlier pass this same day
  > removed "Maluri" entirely, reasoning that Search Console shows **zero impressions** for any
  > query containing it. That was wrong on two counts, and the correction came from the client's
  > own knowledge of the area:
  >
  > 1. **Impressions are demand × our visibility.** A zero cannot distinguish "nobody searches
  >    this" from "we don't rank for this". It is not evidence of absent demand.
  > 2. **GSC cannot see assistant queries at all.** Someone asking ChatGPT or Gemini
  >    "chiropractor near Maluri" produces no impression in that data. This project's brief is
  >    AEO and GEO as much as SEO, so a search-only instrument cannot be used to retire a term
  >    that is used conversationally.
  >
  > Maluri is the colloquial name locals say out loud — Sunway Velocity sits on the
  > Cheras/Maluri boundary and Maluri is the MRT/LRT interchange. Do not remove it again on
  > impression data alone.

  > **⚠️ THE `aggregateRating` HALF OF THIS LINE IS WITHDRAWN — 2026-08-10. DO NOT IMPLEMENT IT.**
  >
  > Google's structured-data policy excludes **self-serving reviews**: a rating about an
  > entity, published on that entity's own site, is not eligible for review snippets on
  > `LocalBusiness` or `Organization` types. Adding it buys no stars in the SERP and puts a
  > structured-data manual action in play on a YMYL medical site.
  >
  > This is a live temptation rather than a hypothetical one: `googleReviews` in
  > `lib/clinic.ts` already holds `verified: true`, 5.0 from 224 reviews, gated and ready.
  > That data is there to render **on the page** as social proof, which is fine and which the
  > site does. Marking it up is the part that is not.
  >
  > The rating already reaches searchers through the Business Profile, which is where Google
  > wants to source it. Nothing is lost by leaving it out of the JSON-LD.

### Phase 2 — Local SEO (the real "chiropractor near me" lever, weeks 1–4)
"Near me" + the local pack (which sits at position 1–3 of every Cheras SERP) are won off-site:
- **Google Business Profile**: complete category (Chiropractor), Maluri service area, weekly posts, Q&A seeded, photos.
- **Review engine**: systematic post-visit review asks. Local pack ranking ≈ reviews × proximity × relevance.
- **Citations**: Yelp MY, ClassPass (Connect Chiropractic ranks via ClassPass), Foursquare, Waze/Google Maps, TripAdvisor health, local MY directories — exact NAP match.
