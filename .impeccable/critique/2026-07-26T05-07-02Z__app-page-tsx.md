## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored at the atomic level, generic at the compositional level.

Real fingerprints: the vertebral mark (derived from the logo AND the technique — Gonstead finds one segment, the mark is a stack of segments), and the 9rem asymmetric hero corner cut, used once. But the band sequence is the canonical local-service template. Seven of nine bands are structurally identical: Eyebrow -> h2 -> card grid. No band breaks form to make a point.

The mark is being diluted: DESIGN.md says it prefixes section labels, which is what makes it describe the method. Here it also appears 4x as standalone card ornament with no label, twice in gold, in bands that ask for no action. /img/hero-adjustment.webp renders twice, ~2 mobile screens apart.

Deterministic scan: 8 findings. The homepage render tree is COMPLETELY CLEAN (page.tsx, ui.tsx, Header.tsx, Footer.tsx, Preloader.tsx = zero). 7 design-system-color hits are in GoogleReviews.tsx (not imported by homepage), all confirmed false positives against the documented Platform Mark Exception. 1 side-tab warning is .post-body blockquote — a pull-quote rule, not a card, false against the rule as written.

That pattern is itself the finding: this page's problems are behavioural and structural, not mechanical slop.

Detector caught what the design review missed: 8 undocumented avatar colours in lib/sample-reviews.ts (#1a73e8, #a142f4, #e8710a...), several cool, against the Warm Neutral Rule. Platform Mark Exception is closed by its own wording and does not reach decorative avatar fills. Real drift, mitigated by that file being placeholder data.

## Overall Impression

Craft is well above 24/40; conversion design is well below it. The interaction budget is spent with unusual discipline (native details FAQ so answers ship in static HTML alongside faqSchema; focus-within submenus; one client component). Then nobody walked the page as a person in pain holding a phone.

Biggest opportunity: this page ranks and converts worse than /services/back-pain, which has a sticky CTA, a Google-styled review block, and a shared CtaBand. The money page is missing all three.

## What's Working

1. Band-plus-hairline structure. border-y border-line at #e5dfd4 instead of shadows: every boundary unambiguous at any scroll position with no elevation cue. Why nine bands still scan cleanly.
2. Interaction budget. Native details for FAQ and drawer, CSS-only submenus, one client component. Correct trade for a CWV-dependent SEO rebuild.
3. Copy refuses to promise and reads more credible for it. homeIntro.body[1] — "including the parts of your case that care is unlikely to change" — is the most differentiating sentence on the site. Constraint became voice.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] On a phone, there is no booking action for roughly five screens

Header.tsx:105 wraps GoldButton in hidden sm:block. sm = 640px; every phone is below it. Homepage does not render StickyCta, which exists at components/service.tsx:207 and IS used on /services/*. Between the hero button and the gold band at line 262 there is no visible booking affordance; only route is hamburger -> 22-row drawer -> Book pinned at bottom.

Why: primary user is on a phone, in pain, comparing three clinics. She gets a better conversion surface on a service page than on the page that ranks.

Fix: render StickyCta on the homepage; replace header CTA below sm with a 44px gold icon-button beside the hamburger.

Command: /impeccable adapt

### [P1] Peak-end inverted — the page ends on a blog trio

Verified band order: hero(43) accreditations(97) intro(116) care paths(163) testimonials(236) CTA band(262) FAQ(293) Visit us(335) blog(379). The ask fires before the two bands that resolve blocking objections, and after they resolve the page never asks again. DESIGN.md specifies the Conversion Band sits at the foot of every content page; it is mid-page here.

Fix: reorder to hero -> accreditations -> intro -> care paths -> FAQ -> Visit us -> reviews -> CtaBand (foot). Move blog above it or cut it.

Command: /impeccable layout

### [P1] The money page carries the weakest social proof on the site

Two quotes, no rating, no source, no date, bare first names. First credits "Dr Derek", not on the roster — already flagged in lib/home.ts:47 as migrated-as-is pending client confirmation. Meanwhile GoogleReviews.tsx (full GBP-styled block) renders on service pages but not here.

Why: she just came from a competitor's Google listing showing a rating. Two anonymous paragraphs, one about a doctor who does not work there, reads as "this clinic has two happy patients".

Fix: resolve Dr Derek with the clinic; give the homepage the same social-proof component the service pages use so it inherits real numbers on verification.

Command: /impeccable layout

### [P1] The preloader taxes exactly the visitor the page exists for

REVEAL_MS = 2950 with document.body.style.overflow = hidden, gated to entryPath === '/' — the definition of an organic search arrival. No skip. prefers-reduced-motion and session key ARE handled correctly.

Why: three seconds of enforced stillness on a brand gesture while a person in pain has three tabs open.

Fix: cut to ~1200ms (pulse once not twice), do not lock scroll, add tap-to-dismiss. If it cannot go below ~1.5s, gate to min-width 1024px.

Command: /impeccable animate

### [P2] Fear is answered nowhere a frightened person will find it

Hero is a photograph of an adjustment being performed — the thing a first-timer fears — and it contradicts positioning claim #2 (thoroughness BEFORE contact). /img/consultation-assessment.webp already exists and states the positioning. "Do chiropractic adjustments hurt?" is the 4th of 4 collapsed FAQs at ~78% scroll. No fear-answering sentence above that point.

Fix: swap the hero image (also fixes the duplicate-photo problem), rewrite offers[1].body into two plain sentences, add under the hero CTAs: "First visit is an assessment — 30-60 minutes. Nothing is adjusted before we've examined you."

Command: /impeccable clarify
